Geri always has a goal in mind. When I got to know him at the age of 16 he already finished a successful car racer career and just started his new job at the local mall's bakery. Shortly, in a couple of years period he also tried various roles such as courier, warehouse worker and print operator of a sportswear company. He never stopped tinkering. From flat-renovation to buggy car building (yes, a real car from scratch!) he was always busy with something. So I was not very surprised when he announced one day that he bought a crappy tourist bus for 3000 EURs. The plan was to convert it to a camper. So what's the big deal here?

Since I really enjoy unconventional projects, I was helping out with some minor tasks on the bus when Geri asked for my honest opinion: **whether it was even a good idea to take on this project?**

He was in a low point as the repair took longer and ate up more money than he expected, and real hesitation appeared on his face. If you've ever thrown yourself into something completely new, then I guess you can imagine how it ends - a lot of suffering and a serious financial loss. And that is exactly what happened with a bunch of his former projects: the overall cost of the buggy was around double what he eventually sold it for. But the main difference with those cases is that he never really cared. It wasn't about money. It wasn't about impressing anyone. Just pure joy and exploration. So the first thing came to my mind was, what changed?
