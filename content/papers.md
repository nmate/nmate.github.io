## Papers
- **[Industrial-scale Stateless Network Functions](/papers/industrial-scale-ieee-cloud-2019.pdf)**

    Once you move your services from dedicated hardware (e.g. DSP, Digital Signal Processor) to commodity (e.g. VM, Virtual Machine) you will face some unexpected problems. In particular, when a DSP goes down, you lose as many sessions as a DSP can handle - which is a dozen. Along the same lines, in the new era, if a VM kicks the bucket it may bring down thousands of sessions that is pretty much unacceptable. The paper tells you how to handle this problem.

    *Published in: IEEE Cloud, Milan, Italy, 2019.*
